A calmer way to browse.

  1. Save what catches your eye.

    Press Ctrl + Alt + P while focused on a webpage in any Chrome window in this profile. You’ll see a confirmation after it’s stored. The library can be in another window, or closed. The shortcut also works inside embedded pages and saves the main tab.

  2. Let topics take shape.

    Shared words in titles and descriptions create topic groups. Click a topic title to rename it, create an empty topic in advance, or drag a link onto another topic card.

  3. Make room in your tab bar.

    After saving, you can close the original tab yourself. Pin a saved link to keep favorites at the top. Hover or focus a title to preview its captured content.

  4. Keep a backup.

    Your library survives restarts. Export a backup before uninstalling the extension, deleting your Chrome profile, or moving devices.

Ctrl + Alt + P requires webpage focus. With the address bar focused, use Ctrl + Shift + P (configurable at chrome://extensions/shortcuts). Chrome settings, the Web Store, and other protected pages can’t be captured. Separate Chrome profiles and other browsers have separate libraries. Previews are saved excerpts, not live pages.
